🏛️ About the 7-Minute Government Typing Test Simulation

State government examination boards, court stenographer commissions, and commercial typing institutes (including GCC-TBC in Maharashtra) standardly conduct speed assessments with strict 7-minute time limits. This practice test provides a 1:1 replica of the official exam environment.

🎯

30 WPM Target (Clerk Grade)

Requires typing 210+ words in 7 minutes (1,050 keystrokes) with at least 90% Net accuracy.

⚡

40 WPM Target (Senior Typist)

Requires typing 280+ words in 7 minutes (1,400 keystrokes) with error deduction penalties.

📊

Net WPM & Marks Calculation

Scored on a 50-mark scale where each uncorrected mistake incurs deductions against your gross speed.
